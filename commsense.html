Behavioral Profile

Five dimensions of communication style

Every person communicates differently. CommSense uses AI-native analysis to classify interactions across five key behavioral dimensions — giving individuals and managers a nuanced view that goes far beyond simple sentiment.

  • Assertiveness — High, Medium, or Low engagement patterns
  • Conflict & Feedback — Collaborating, Accommodating, or Competing
  • Information Sharing — Open, Selective, or Minimal transparency
Relationship Scoring

Core Capabilities

What powers CommSense

Platform-Agnostic Identity

One person, one profile — regardless of how many tools they use. CommSense resolves identities across Slack, Teams, Email, and Calendar into a unified communication graph.

Privacy by Design

Employees see only their own data. Managers see only their granted scope. No surveillance — just intelligence that drives improvement, with strict access controls baked in from day one.

AI-Native Analysis

Classification, scoring, and anomaly detection powered by LLMs operating directly over the communication graph — not simple keyword matching or rule-based heuristics.

Signal Over Noise

In group chats, multi-recipient emails, and large meetings, CommSense extracts genuine interaction signals from co-participation noise — surfacing what actually matters.

Multi-Platform Integration

Connects to Microsoft Graph (Teams, Mail, Calendar), Slack, and future integrations — pulling all organizational communication into a single unified analysis engine.

Anomaly Detection

Automatic alerts when individuals communicate with certain people markedly differently than others, when volume spikes or drops, or when team dynamics shift unexpectedly.
